## UPM responsibilities

- Reference the exact EBMA specification and profile.

- Record gaps and approved exceptions with traceability.
- Keep manifest version and source revision immutable and identifiable.

## Evidence requirements

A manifest's `conformance` section is a declaration of evidence references and state, not self-certification. A verifier must inspect evidence, scope, integrity, recency and applicability.
